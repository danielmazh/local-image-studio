Open the portal

The studio is off. Nothing runs on the Mac and no memory is used.
Your tabs, chats and history are saved.

To start it, run in a terminal:

img-on

The portal opens by itself. This page also switches to it once the studio is running.

Commands: img-on start and open the portal · img-off stop everything · img-status show state · img-portal open the portal again · img-home this page.

Full guide: Help tab in the portal, or README.md in the project folder.
